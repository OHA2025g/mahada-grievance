MHADA Grievance Management and Redressal - UI Wireframe Design Pack
Design proposal v0.1 | 14 October 2026

113 full-size views: 101 main screens and 12 parent-linked dialogs.

Contents
- png/: 113 artboards, 1440 x 970 pixels.
- svg/: 113 editable vector artboards; ID names match the viewer and PDF.
- MHADA_Grievance_Wireframes.html: portable linked viewer. Open locally in a modern browser.
- MHADA_Screen_Inventory.csv: workspaces, purposes, transitions, required fields and PDF pages.
- MHADA_Screen_Definitions.json: complete field definitions, rules, tables and six journeys.

Review
Use All screens, workspace/screen pickers or Journeys. Click canvas actions to follow routes.
Use Edit fields or click a field to change the local preview; edits last only for the session.
No upload, notification, transaction or portal operation occurs.
Preview states: normal, empty, error, loading, denied.
SVG is the vector interchange format; it is not a native Figma component library.

Data and policy
Individual records, people, documents, properties and appointments are fictional.
Historical aggregates are explicitly dated to analysis of the supplied exports on 14 October 2026.
Source status is separate from internal work, outcome, priority, feedback and technical delivery.
Confirm applicable MHADA policy, holidays, authority, deadlines and challenge mechanisms.

Design handoff
The main artboards are desktop layouts. Responsive, language, accessible and role/case permission
behaviour is specified in the PDF handoff; production implementation and testing remain required.

Validation
Inventory, field visibility, bounds, target IDs, reachability, PDF and image rendering checked.
Browser execution and production integrations were unavailable for verification.
